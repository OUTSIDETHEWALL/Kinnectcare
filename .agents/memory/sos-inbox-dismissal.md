---
name: SOS inbox dismissal
description: Local resolved-notification dismissal is separate from incident history, emergency status and retention.
---

Clear All dismisses explicitly resolved SOS notifications in this installation's
signed-in account/family inbox. It is not a family-synchronized dismissal, incident
deletion, acknowledgment or resolution. Each phone dismisses independently;
clearing app storage or reinstalling may restore locally dismissed notifications.

**Why:** The owner approved separating a caregiver's notification cleanup from
shared emergency evidence and the existing 30-day resolved-history policy.

**How to apply:** Missing/false resolution remains active even after acknowledgment.
A reopened incident overrides old dismissal, and a new resolution is separately
dismissible. Preserve SOS history, its original resolution time and existing
retention. Keep ordinary-alert family-wide deletion distinct in confirmations.
Automated multi-phone models do not replace separately authorized physical
acceptance on both phones.
